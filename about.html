Meet the Doctor

Spanish tutor, Ph.D., and lifelong language enthusiast — available in Northern Virginia and via Zoom.

Ricardo

Ph.D. in Spanish Language & Literature

I hold a Ph.D. in Spanish Language & Literature and have been teaching and tutoring Spanish for over a decade. My approach is rooted in real communication — not rote memorization — and I adapt every session to your specific goals, whether that's nailing an exam, surviving a family reunion in Spanish, or unlocking the next level of fluency.

I'm based in Northern Virginia and offer sessions in person or via Zoom, so geography is never the obstacle.

Beyond the classroom

Outside of tutoring, I run a YouTube channel dedicated to intermediate and advanced Spanish learners — covering grammar deep-dives, cultural insights, and vocabulary that textbooks tend to skip. I also blog here for speakers who are ready to go beyond the basics.

For a fuller picture of who I am beyond Spanish, visit my personal site.
